moko includes independent open-source software next to its own code, which is licensed under the GNU General Public License version 3 or later. The application includes PostgreSQL, PHP and, in builds that say "mariadb · included" in settings → components, MariaDB Server. Each server runs as its own process; the application talks to it over a local connection.
